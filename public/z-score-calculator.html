What is a z-score?

The z-score, also called the standard score, z-value or normal score, is a dimensionless number that shows how many standard deviations a data point is above or below the mean. Values above the mean have positive z-scores, and values below the mean have negative z-scores.

To find it, subtract the population mean from the raw score (a test score, height, age and so on), then divide the difference by the population standard deviation:

z = (x − μ) / σ

Here x is the raw score, μ is the population mean and σ is the population standard deviation. For a sample, the formula is the same except that the sample mean and sample standard deviation are used.

Z-scores are used in z-tests, prediction intervals, process control, and for comparing scores measured on different scales.

Z-score example

A test has a mean of 3 and a standard deviation of 2. A student scores 5.

z = (5 − 3) / 2 = 1

The score is exactly 1 standard deviation above the mean. In the converter, P(x<1) ≈ 0.8413, so the student scored higher than about 84% of test takers.

How to read the z-table

  • The row headings give the z-score to the tenths place.
  • The column headings give the hundredths place.
  • Each value is the area between z = 0 and that z-score, which is the probability that a data point lies in that region of the standard normal distribution.

For example, a z-score of 1.12 corresponds to an area of 0.36864 (row 1.1, column 0.02). So for a normally distributed population there is a 36.864% chance that a data point has a z-score between 0 and 1.12.

Because there are several kinds of z-tables, always check which area the table is showing.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate a z-score?

Subtract the population mean (μ) from the raw score (x), then divide by the population standard deviation (σ): z = (x − μ) / σ.

What does a z-score of 0 mean?

The value is exactly equal to the mean, at the center of the standard normal distribution.

How do you convert a z-score to a probability?

Look the z-score up in a z-table or use the converter above. The result is an area under the standard normal curve, for example P(Z < 1.96) ≈ 0.975.

Can a z-score be negative?

Yes. A negative z-score means the value is below the mean, and a positive one means it is above the mean.

What z-score is used for a 95% confidence interval?

A 95% two-sided confidence interval uses a critical z-value of about 1.96.
