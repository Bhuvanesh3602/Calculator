Real Estate Calculator FAQs

Quick answers to the questions people ask most about real estate calculators.

What is a real estate calculator?

A real estate calculator is an online tool that estimates the numbers behind buying, financing, renting, or improving property, such as monthly mortgage payments, affordability, loan payoff time, rental returns, and material quantities. The results are estimates to help you plan.

Which real estate calculator should I use first when buying a home?

Start with the House Affordability Calculator to see a realistic price range for your income and debts. Then use the Mortgage Calculator to estimate monthly payments and the Down Payment Calculator to see how different down payments change the loan.

How much house can I afford?

A common guideline is the 28/36 rule: housing costs of no more than about 28% of gross monthly income, and total debt payments of no more than about 36%. Lenders look at your credit, savings, and loan type too, so treat the rule as a starting point. The House Affordability Calculator applies these ratios to your numbers.

How do I calculate a monthly mortgage payment?

The payment is based on the loan amount, interest rate, and loan term. Most buyers also pay property tax, homeowners insurance, and sometimes private mortgage insurance (PMI) or HOA dues. The Mortgage Calculator includes these extra costs so the total is closer to what you would really pay.

How much should my down payment be?

Putting down 20% usually avoids private mortgage insurance on a conventional loan, but many buyers put down less. FHA loans allow as little as 3.5% down for qualified borrowers, and VA loans may require no down payment for eligible veterans and service members. Try different amounts in the Down Payment Calculator.

What is the difference between an FHA loan and a VA loan?

FHA loans are insured by the Federal Housing Administration and are open to a wide range of buyers, including those with lower credit scores or smaller down payments. VA loans are backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs and are only for eligible veterans, service members, and some surviving spouses. Compare them with the FHA Loan Calculator and the VA Mortgage Calculator.

Is it worth refinancing my mortgage?

It can be if the new rate is meaningfully lower or the new term fits your goals, but closing costs matter. Divide the closing costs by your monthly savings to find the break-even point, and refinance only if you plan to stay in the home longer than that. The Refinance Calculator runs the comparison.

How can I pay off my mortgage faster?

Extra monthly payments, one-time lump sums, or switching to biweekly payments all cut the principal sooner and reduce total interest. Use the Mortgage Payoff Calculator to see how many years and how much interest you would save.

What is the difference between a home equity loan and a HELOC?

A home equity loan pays out a lump sum with a fixed payment schedule. A HELOC is a revolving line of credit you can draw from as needed, usually with a variable rate. Both use your home as collateral. See the Home Equity Loan Calculator and the HELOC Calculator.

What is APR, and how is it different from the interest rate?

The interest rate is the cost of borrowing the principal. The APR (annual percentage rate) also folds in fees and points, so it reflects the loan's overall yearly cost. It is the better number for comparing loan offers. The APR Calculator calculates it.

How do I know if a rental property is a good investment?

Investors look at monthly cash flow, the capitalization rate (net operating income divided by property value), and return on the cash they put in. A positive cash flow after all expenses and a cap rate that fits your market are common signs of a workable deal. The Rental Property Calculator shows all three.

Should I rent or buy a home?

It depends on how long you plan to stay, local prices compared with rents, mortgage rates, and what your savings could earn elsewhere. The Rent vs. Buy Calculator shows how many years you would need to stay for buying to cost less, and the Rent Calculator checks whether a rent fits your income.

How do I estimate materials for a roofing, tile, or concrete project?

Measure the surface, then enter the dimensions into the right tool. The Roofing Calculator, Tile Calculator, and Concrete Calculator estimate quantities, and the Area Calculator and Square Footage Calculator help with measurements. Add extra for waste and cuts.

Are these real estate calculators free and accurate?

They are free to use. They give estimates based on the numbers you enter and standard formulas, so actual figures from a lender, appraiser, or contractor may differ. They are not financial or legal advice.
