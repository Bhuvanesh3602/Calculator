Example 1: Simple tip

A meal costs $55 before tax and you want to leave a 15% tip.

tip = 55 × 0.15 = $8.25
total = 55 + 8.25 = $63.25

Example 2: Splitting the bill

A group of 4 people has a $120 bill and agrees on an 18% tip.

tip = 120 × 0.18 = $21.60
total = 120 + 21.60 = $141.60
tip per person = $5.40
total per person = $35.40

Tip in Your Head

Move the decimal point one place to the left to find 10% of the bill. For 20%, double that amount. For 15%, add half of the 10% amount to it. On a $60 bill, 10% is $6, 20% is $12 and 15% is $9.

About Tipping

A tip or gratuity is an extra sum of money paid to certain service workers for a provided service. Tip amounts, as well as acceptance, vary in different parts of the world. In some countries in East Asia, such as Japan, tips are seen as insulting and can sometimes be interpreted as a bribe. In yet other countries, such as the United States, tipping is widely expected, and in many cases, is even factored into a service worker's compensation towards satisfying the minimum wage requirement. This is important to note, since although tipping is entirely voluntary, many servers depend on tips to make a living in countries like the United States. As such, as a tourist, it can be helpful to research the tipping customs in the countries being visited. In the U.S. or any other country where tipping is expected, depending on the restaurant or the number of patrons at a table, gratuity may be automatically applied to the bill amount. As previously mentioned, tipping can be offensive in some countries, so although a citizen of the United States that is visiting another country may want to express their appreciation of the service provided, the gesture may, in some cases, result in the opposite effect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question to see the answer.

How do you calculate a tip?

Multiply the bill by the tip percentage divided by 100. For example, a 15% tip on a $55 bill is 55 × 0.15 = $8.25, so the total is $63.25.


How much should I tip at a restaurant?

In the United States and Canada, a tip of 15% to 20% of the pre-tax bill is typical for restaurants and bartenders. Many people tip 18% to 20% for good service.


Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?

A tip is typically calculated on the before-tax price of the meal. Some people tip on the total including tax, which gives a slightly larger tip. Both are acceptable.


How do you split a bill with a tip?

Add the tip to the bill to get the total, then divide by the number of people. For a $120 bill with an 18% tip split between 4 people, the tip is $21.60, the total is $141.60, and each person pays $35.40.


How can I calculate a 15% or 20% tip quickly in my head?

Find 10% by moving the decimal point one place to the left. For 20%, double that amount. For 15%, add half of the 10% amount to the 10% amount. On a $60 bill, 10% is $6, 20% is $12, and 15% is $9.


Is the tip already included in the bill?

Sometimes. Depending on the restaurant or the size of the group, a gratuity or service charge may be automatically added to the bill. Check the bill before adding an extra tip.


Is tipping expected in every country?

No. Tipping is widely expected in the United States, but in some countries, such as Japan, tipping is not customary and can even be seen as insulting. It helps to research local customs before you travel.


How much should I tip for food delivery, taxis and hair services?

Typical amounts in the United States and Canada are 15% to 20% for food delivery and taxi or limo drivers, and 10% to 20% for hairstylists, barbers, nail services and massage.
