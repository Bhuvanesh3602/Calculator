Calculate the duration between two times

Calculating the duration between two times can be a little tricky depending on the numbers of minutes and seconds in the two times being compared. As an example, the following are the steps to determine the number of hours and minutes between two chosen times within the same day:

  1. First, identify the starting and an ending time. The goal is to subtract the starting time from the ending time under the correct conditions.
  2. If the times are not already in 24-hour time, convert them to 24-hour time. AM hours are the same in both 12-hour and 24-hour time (except 12:00 AM, which is 00:00). For PM hours, add 12 to the number to convert it to 24-hour time. For example, 1:00 PM would be 13:00 in 24-hour time.
  3. Determine whether the number of minutes is larger in the starting time or the ending time.
    • If the ending time has a larger number of minutes: Subtract the starting time from the ending time as you would subtract normally, keeping the hours and minutes on the appropriate side of the ":", where hours are on the left, and minutes are on the right. For example:
       13:57
      – 9:22
       4:35
    • If the starting time has a larger number of minutes: Treat the hour and minute portion separately. Add 60 to the number of minutes in the ending time, and subtract 1 hour from the hour portion of the ending time. Then, subtract the minutes and the hours, keeping the result on the appropriate side of the ":" where hours are on the left, and minutes are on the right. For example:
       13:57
      – 9:58
      		  ⇒  
       12:117
      – 9:58  
       3:59  

Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question to see the answer.

How do you calculate the time between two times?

Convert both times to 24-hour format and subtract the start time from the end time. If the end time has fewer minutes than the start time, borrow 1 hour by adding 60 to the end minutes. For example, 13:57 minus 9:58 becomes 12:117 minus 9:58, which is 3 hours 59 minutes.


How do you convert 12-hour time to 24-hour time?

For AM times from 1:00 to 11:59, the hours stay the same. For PM times from 1:00 to 11:59, add 12 to the hour, so 1:00 PM is 13:00. Midnight (12:00 AM) is 00:00 and noon (12:00 PM) is 12:00.


How do you calculate the duration when the time passes midnight?

Add 24 hours to the end time before subtracting. For example, from 22:00 to 06:00 the next day, use 30:00 minus 22:00, which is 8 hours. You can also use the Time Between Two Dates calculator and enter each date.


How do you find the time between two dates?

Use the Time Between Two Dates calculator on this page. Enter the start date and time and the end date and time, and it gives the number of days, hours, minutes and seconds between them.


How many minutes and seconds are in an hour?

There are 60 minutes in an hour and 60 seconds in a minute, so 1 hour is 3,600 seconds. For example, 9 hours is 540 minutes or 32,400 seconds.


How do you convert hours and minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60 and add the result to the hours. For example, 5 hours 30 minutes is 5.5 hours, and 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75 hours.


Can I include seconds in the calculation?

Yes. Both calculators have Hour, Minute and Second fields, so you can find the duration down to the second.
