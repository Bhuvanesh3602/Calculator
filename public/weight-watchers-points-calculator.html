What are food points and how do they work?

A points system turns a food's nutrition label into one easy number. Foods higher in calories, sugar, or saturated fat cost more points, while protein and fiber lower the cost in some versions. You then stay within a daily points budget instead of counting every nutrient.

How are the points calculated?

SystemYearsEstimate formula
Original pointsBefore 2010Calories ÷ 50 + fat ÷ 12 − fiber ÷ 5 (fiber max 4 g)
PointsPlus2010–2015Protein ÷ 10.9375 + carbs ÷ 9.2105 + fat ÷ 3.8928 − fiber ÷ 12.5 (fiber max 4 g)
SmartPoints2015–2021Calories × 0.0305 + sat. fat × 0.275 + sugar × 0.12 − protein × 0.098

Amounts are in grams. Results are rounded to the nearest whole point and never go below zero.

Worked example

A snack with 250 calories, 4 g saturated fat, 10 g sugar, and 8 g protein: 250 × 0.0305 = 7.63; 4 × 0.275 = 1.10; 10 × 0.12 = 1.20; 8 × 0.098 = 0.78. So 7.63 + 1.10 + 1.20 − 0.78 ≈ 9.14, about 9 SmartPoints.

What changed in the newer programs?

Since 2022 the program uses a different, personalized method that weighs factors such as fiber, protein, unsaturated fat, added sugar, and saturated fat. Its exact math is not public, so no independent calculator can reproduce it. For today's points, use your official program.

Frequently asked questions

How does the Weight Watchers points system work?

Each food gets a points value based on its nutrition, and each person gets a daily points budget. Lower-point foods let you eat more for the same budget.

How are SmartPoints calculated?

SmartPoints ≈ calories × 0.0305 + saturated fat (g) × 0.275 + sugar (g) × 0.12 − protein (g) × 0.098, rounded to the nearest whole number.

How are PointsPlus values calculated?

PointsPlus ≈ protein / 10.9375 + carbohydrates / 9.2105 + fat / 3.8928 − fiber / 12.5, with fiber capped at 4 g. The result is rounded.

How were the original points calculated?

Original points ≈ calories / 50 + fat (g) / 12 − fiber (g) / 5, with fiber capped at 4 g.

Does this calculator show my current points?

No. The current program uses a newer personalized method that is not published, so this tool only estimates the older SmartPoints, PointsPlus and original systems.

What is the SmartPoints value of 250 calories, 4 g saturated fat, 10 g sugar and 8 g protein?

About 9 points: 7.6 + 1.1 + 1.2 − 0.8 = 9.1, which rounds to 9.

Why does my result differ from a food label or app?

Differences come from rounding, the serving size you entered, the fiber cap, and the program version. Always check which version your plan uses.

Are fruits and vegetables zero points?

Programs set their own zero-point food lists, and these lists change. This calculator only applies a formula, so use your program's list for zero-point foods.

How many points should I eat per day?

Daily budgets depend on the program and your personal details. This tool does not set one, so use your program or ask a doctor or dietitian.
