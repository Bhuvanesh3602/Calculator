What is a percentage?

In mathematics, a percentage is a number or ratio that represents a fraction of 100. It is one of the ways to represent a dimensionless relationship between two numbers; other methods include ratios, fractions, and decimals. Percentages are often denoted by the symbol "%" written after the number. They can also be denoted by writing "percent" or "pct" after the number. For example, 35% is equivalent to the decimal 0.35, or the fractions .

Percentages are computed by multiplying the value of a ratio by 100. For example, if 25 out of 50 students in a classroom are male, . The value of the ratio is therefore 0.5, and multiplying this by 100 yields:

0.5 × 100 = 50

In other words, the ratio of 25 males to students in the classroom is equivalent to 50% of students in the classroom being male.

Percentage formula

Although the percentage formula can be written in different forms, it is essentially an algebraic equation involving three values.

P × V1 = V2

P is the percentage, V1 is the first value that the percentage will modify, and V2 is the result of the percentage operating on V1. Percentage change formula

Percentage increase and decrease are calculated by computing the difference between two values and comparing that difference to the initial value. Mathematically, this involves using the absolute value of the difference between two values then dividing the result by the initial value, essentially calculating how much the initial value has changed.

Percentage Calculator – Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate a percentage?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. Percentage = (part ÷ whole) × 100. For example, 25 out of 50 is 25 ÷ 50 = 0.5, and 0.5 × 100 = 50%.

How do you find X% of a number?

Convert the percentage to a decimal by dividing by 100, then multiply by the number. For example, 20% of 50 = 0.20 × 50 = 10.

How do you find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the first number by the second and multiply by 100. For example, 15 is what percent of 60? 15 ÷ 60 × 100 = 25%.

How do you find a number when you know a percentage of it?

Divide the known value by the percentage written as a decimal. For example, if 30 is 20% of a number, the number is 30 ÷ 0.20 = 150.

How do you calculate percentage increase?

Percentage increase = (new value − old value) ÷ old value × 100. For example, going from 80 to 100 is (100 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = 25% increase. To increase a number by a percentage, multiply it by (1 + percentage as a decimal): 500 increased by 10% is 500 × 1.1 = 550.

How do you calculate percentage decrease?

Percentage decrease = (old value − new value) ÷ old value × 100. For example, going from 100 to 80 is (100 − 80) ÷ 100 × 100 = 20% decrease. To decrease a number by a percentage, multiply it by (1 − percentage as a decimal): 500 decreased by 10% is 500 × 0.9 = 450.

What is the difference between percentage change and percentage difference?

Percentage change compares a new value with an original value and divides by the original. Percentage difference compares two values that have no original or reference, and divides by their average: |V1 − V2| ÷ ((V1 + V2) ÷ 2) × 100. For example, the percentage difference between 10 and 6 is 4 ÷ 8 × 100 = 50%. If you are comparing a measurement with a known true value, use the percent error calculator.

How do you calculate a discount or sale price?

Multiply the original price by (1 − discount as a decimal). For example, 25% off $80 is 80 × 0.75 = $60, so you save $20.

How do you convert a percent to a decimal or a fraction?

To get a decimal, divide the percent by 100 (35% = 0.35). To get a fraction, put the percent over 100 and simplify (35% = 35/100 = 7/20). To go back to a percent, multiply the decimal by 100. For fraction simplification, see the fraction calculator.

How do I calculate a percentage for marks or a test score?

Divide the marks obtained by the total marks and multiply by 100. For example, 432 out of 500 is 432 ÷ 500 × 100 = 86.4%.

Can a percentage be greater than 100%?

Yes. A percentage above 100% means the part is larger than the reference value. For example, 150 is 150% of 100, and a quantity that doubles has increased by 100%.

What is the difference between percent and percentage points?

Percentage points describe the simple arithmetic difference between two percentages, while percent describes a relative change. If an interest rate rises from 10% to 15%, it has risen by 5 percentage points, which is a 50% increase.
