What Is a Dice?

A die is a small, throwable object with several faces (most commonly six) that each indicate a number or symbol, used to generate random numbers and events. Dice are central to many tabletop games, including backgammon, Boggle and Yahtzee, and appear in others such as Monopoly, Risk, Dungeons and Dragons and Settlers of Catan. They are also used in gambling.

Dice Shapes

The most commonly used dice shapes are shown in the image and listed below.

  • Tetrahedron: 4 faces (d4), the blue die
  • Cube: 6 faces (d6), the orange, cubic die
  • Octahedron: 8 faces (d8), the green die
  • Pentagonal trapezohedron: 10 faces (d10), the orange, non-cubic die
  • Dodecahedron: 12 faces (d12), the yellow die
  • Icosahedron: 20 faces (d20), the purple die

How Random Is a Dice?

A fair die should have an equal probability of landing on each face. Mass-produced dice are not perfect, though: they are hard to make uniform, and small differences in symmetry can bias the result. Dice such as the d20 and d8 are often slightly unbalanced and more likely to roll certain numbers.

How to Test Your Dice

This quick test is not perfectly accurate, but it needs only a container, water and salt:

  1. Get a container that can fit the die you want to test.
  2. Fill it with water, then add salt and the die. If the die does not float, add more salt until it does.
  3. Flick the die, note which side faces up, and repeat many times, recording the results.

A well-balanced die gives a variety of numbers, while an unbalanced one shows certain numbers more often. Unless you repeat the test many times or the die is heavily unbalanced, you are unlikely to see a significant difference.

Tests on dice from different manufacturers (mostly d20s) found that even dice from the same company under the same conditions can vary significantly and are not truly random. Some companies' dice were more random than others, but none were found to be perfectly random.

Dice Roller FAQ

How do I roll dice online?

Choose how many dice to roll with the number box or slider, then press Roll Dice. The roller shows each die and, when you roll more than one, the sum and product. For other dice such as a d20, use the custom roller and set the number of sides.

How many dice can I roll at once?

The standard roller rolls 1 to 100 six-sided dice at once. The custom roller can roll up to 10,000 dice with any number of sides.

Is this virtual dice roller random?

Each result is generated with your browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues) when available, and falls back to Math.random() otherwise. Every face has an equal chance, and past rolls do not affect future ones.

How do I roll a d20?

Open the custom roller, set Sides on a Dice to 20 and Number of Dice to 1, then press Start. Press Stop to lock in the result. You can also use the d20 quick button.

What are the most common dice used in tabletop games?

The standard polyhedral set is d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 and d20. Games such as Dungeons & Dragons also use a percentile die (d100), made by rolling two d10s.

What is the probability of rolling a 7 with two dice?

Rolling a sum of 7 with two six-sided dice has a 6 in 36 chance, or 16.67%, making it the most likely total. Sums of 2 and 12 are the least likely at 1 in 36 (2.78%) each.

Are physical dice truly random?

Not perfectly. Mass-produced dice can have small imbalances, and studies of d20s have found that even dice from the same maker can differ. A virtual roller avoids manufacturing bias.

How can I test whether my physical die is fair?

A quick test is to float the die in salt water, flick it many times, and record which face ends up on top. A fair die should give a mix of numbers. You need many rolls to see a meaningful difference.
