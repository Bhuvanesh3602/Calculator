How to Calculate Age

Your age is the number of full years between your date of birth and the date you are measuring to. To find it by hand:

  1. Subtract the birth year from the current year.
  2. If your birthday has not happened yet this year, subtract 1.
  3. For months and days, count the full months since your last birthday, then the days left over.

Example

Born on May 15, 2000, and measured on October 25, 2026:

May 15, 2000 → May 15, 2026 = 26 years
May 15 → Oct 15, 2026 = 5 months
Oct 15 → Oct 25, 2026 = 10 days
Age = 26 years, 5 months, 10 days

You can also use the second date field to find your age on any past or future date, for example your age on a future graduation or retirement day, or the interval between any two dates.

How Age Is Counted in Different Cultures

The age of a person can be counted differently in different cultures. This calculator uses the most common system, in which age increases on a person's birthday. For example, a person who has lived 3 years and 11 months is 3, and turns 4 on their next birthday one month later. Most Western countries use this system.

In some cultures, age is expressed by counting years with or without including the current year. A person who is twenty years old is the same age as someone in their twenty-first year of life. In one traditional Chinese age system, people are born at age 1 and gain a year at the Traditional Chinese New Year rather than on their birthday. A baby born one day before the Traditional Chinese New Year would be 2 two days later, even though only 2 days old.

South Korea traditionally used a similar system, but moved to the international age system (age increases on the birthday) for most legal and administrative purposes in June 2023.

Why Months and Days Can Look Confusing at Month-End

In some situations the months and days result can be confusing, especially when the starting date is at the end of a month. We count Feb. 20 to Mar. 20 as one month, but there are two ways to calculate the age from Feb. 28, 2022 to Mar. 31, 2022. If Feb. 28 to Mar. 28 is one month, the result is one month and 3 days. If both Feb. 28 and Mar. 31 are treated as the end of the month, the result is one month. Both are reasonable. Similar cases include Apr. 30 to May 31 and May 30 to June 30. The confusion comes from the uneven number of days in different months. This calculator uses the first method: whole months from the same day of the month, plus the leftover days.

Age Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate my age from my date of birth?

Subtract your birth year from the current year, then subtract one more year if your birthday has not happened yet this year. For an exact age, count the full months and remaining days since your last birthday. The calculator above does this automatically.

How do I calculate age on a specific date?

Enter your date of birth and set the 'Age at the Date of' field to the date you want, in the past or the future. The result is your exact age on that day.

Does the age calculator account for leap years?

Yes. The count uses the real calendar, including leap years and the different lengths of each month, so day counts are exact.

Why do months and days sometimes look odd at the end of a month?

Months have different lengths, so there are two reasonable ways to count from a date such as Feb. 28 to Mar. 31. This calculator counts whole months from the same day of the month and then the leftover days, so Feb. 28 to Mar. 31 is 1 month and 3 days.

How is age counted in different cultures?

Most Western countries increase age on the birthday. In some traditional East Asian systems, a person is 1 at birth and gains a year at the New Year instead. South Korea moved to the international age system for most legal and administrative purposes in June 2023.

What is the age of someone born in a given year?

It is the current year minus the birth year, minus 1 if their birthday has not yet occurred this year. Use the calculator with the exact birth date for a precise answer.

Can I calculate the time between two dates that are not birthdays?

Yes. Put the start date in the first field and the end date in the second to get the interval in years, months, weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds. For adding or subtracting days, see the Date Calculator.
