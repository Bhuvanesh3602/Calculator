Frequently Asked Questions

What is TDEE?

TDEE stands for total daily energy expenditure. It is the total energy a person uses in a day, made up of basal metabolic rate (BMR), physical activity and the thermic effect of food. It is usually estimated rather than measured.

How is TDEE calculated?

First estimate your BMR from age, sex, height and weight, then multiply it by an activity factor. The formula is TDEE = BMR Ã— activity factor. For example, a BMR of 1,655 calories and a moderate activity factor of 1.465 gives about 2,425 calories per day.

When is the Katch-McArdle formula more accurate?

Katch-McArdle uses lean body mass, so it can be more accurate if you are lean and know your body fat percentage. The formula is BMR = 370 + 21.6 Ã— lean body mass (kg). For example, 65 kg at 20% body fat has 52 kg of lean mass, so BMR = 370 + 21.6 Ã— 52 = about 1,493 calories.

Choose the level that matches your typical week. The factors are 1.2 for sedentary, 1.375 for light exercise 1â€“3 times per week, 1.465 for moderate exercise 4â€“5 times per week, 1.55 for daily or intense exercise 3â€“4 times per week, 1.725 for intense exercise 6â€“7 times per week, and 1.9 for very intense daily exercise or a physical job. If you are unsure, pick the lower level.

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR is the minimum energy your body needs at rest to maintain vital functions. TDEE is your BMR plus the energy used for daily activity, exercise and digesting food, so TDEE is always higher than BMR.

How accurate is a TDEE calculator?

A TDEE calculator gives an estimate, not an exact figure, because energy use varies from person to person and from day to day. Use the result as a starting point, and adjust it based on how your weight changes over several weeks. It is not medical advice, so talk to a healthcare professional about your individual needs.

How do I convert calories to kilojoules?

Multiply calories (kcal) by 4.184 to get kilojoules (kJ). For example, 2,425 calories is about 10,146 kJ. In the calculator Settings you can choose Calories or Kilojoules for the results.
